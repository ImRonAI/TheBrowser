### Ryan White HIV/AIDS Program — eligibility verdicts (both verified)
- **Washoe/Reno is NOT Part A-eligible.** Nevada's only Part A area is the **Las Vegas TGA (Clark + Nye Counties NV, plus Mohave County AZ)**; TGA threshold = 1,000–1,999 AIDS cases/5 yrs [source: https://akaprod-hab.hrsa.gov/about-ryan-white-hivaids-program/part-a-grants-emerging-metro-transitional-areas | 2021] [source: https://www.dpbh.nv.gov/siteassets/programs/hiv-ryan/Resource_Guide_2019_Final.pdf | 2019]

### CDC Ending the HIV Epidemic (EHE) — eligibility verdict (verified)
- EHE Phase 1 = 57 jurisdictions: **48 counties + DC + San Juan, PR** (>50% of 2016-17 diagnoses) + **7 rural-burden states (AL, AR, KY, MS, MO, OK, SC)** [source: https://www.cdc.gov/ehe/php/jurisdictions-plans/index.html | 2026] [source: https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/68/wr/mm6825a2.htm | 2019/2023]
- **Nevada is NOT one of the 7 states; Washoe County is NOT among the 48 counties.** Clark County NV IS included [source: https://ahead.hiv.gov/nevada/clark_county/ | accessed 2026] [source: https://www.hiv.gov/federal-response/ending-the-hiv-epidemic/jurisdictions | 2026]
- Fiscal implication: Washoe cannot apply for dedicated EHE jurisdictional funds; federal HIV prevention flows via state DPBH and Part C/FQHC channels. PS19-1906 funded the 57 Phase-1 recipients under 5-year cooperative agreements [source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11417129/ | 2024]

## 4. ROI / Benefit-Cost Evidence (preventive health)

### HIV / PrEP
- LA County MSM model: PrEP **$27,863 per QALY** (cost-effective vs $150K/QALY threshold) [source: https://academic.oup.com/cid/article/63/11/1495/2526220 | 2016]
- Modeling review: PrEP prioritized to high-risk MSM delivers ~**80% of clinical benefit at 15% of the cost** of universal provision [source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4779384/ | 2016]
- Annual PrEP delivery cost ≈ **$1,977/person (2021 USD)**, incl. clinical support [source: https://hivhep.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/PrEP_Cost_Final_Report_21November2022.pdf | 2022]
- Preventing one HIV case saves **>$225K lifetime medical cost** (Schackman 2015: lifetime care cost from infection ≈ $361,400 in then-dollars; the underlying 2006 entry-to-care analysis was $618,900 in 2004 USD) [source: https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2015/02/preventing-one-case-hiv-saves-over-225k-study-shows | 2015] [source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4406522/ | 2015]
- A published BCR definition for PrEP+testing = $ saved in HIV treatment per $1 invested — magnitude depends on epidemic setting (study is China-based; **no US-wide single BCR ratio verified**) [source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12722553/ | 2025]. **A universal "PrEP ROI = X:1" figure: NEEDS-VERIFICATION.**

### Suicide / mental health (untreated-burden costs)
- US national suicide cost **$502.7B (2018/2019)** incl. quality-of-life losses; up 58% from $318.6B (1999/2000) [source: https://bmcpublichealth.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12889-023-15188-8 | 2023]
- Average cost per suicide ≈ **$1.3M** (Shepard et al. 2016; ~$2.0M for ages 15–24): widely cited but **no direct URL retrieved this pass — NEEDS-VERIFICATION**; CDC WISQARS older method put it at ~$859K — **NEEDS-VERIFICATION** [source: reported via Brave Answer aggregation | 2026]
