## 1. LGBTQ POPULATION SIZE & DEMOGRAPHICS

### 1.1 Nevada statewide (primary, verified)
- **150,100 LGBT adults (18+) in Nevada — 6.6% of adults**, per Williams Institute analysis of 2020–2021 BRFSS, published Dec 2023 [source: https://williamsinstitute.law.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/LGBT-Adult-US-Pop-Dec-2023.pdf | 2023]
- **173,900 total LGBTQ population (ages 13+) in Nevada**, combining Williams 2023 (adults), Williams 2020 (LGB youth), Williams 2025 (transgender youth) [source: https://mapresearch.org/equality-profiles/nv/ | 2026 (data as of Nov 11, 2026)]
- **23,800 LGBTQ youth (ages 13–17) in Nevada** [source: https://mapresearch.org/equality-profiles/nv/ | 2026 citing Williams 2020/2025]
- **92,000 total LGBTQ workers in Nevada** [source: https://mapresearch.org/equality-profiles/nv/ | 2026 citing Williams 2020]
- **22% of LGBTQ adults (25+) in Nevada are raising children** [source: https://mapresearch.org/equality-profiles/nv/ | 2026 citing Gallup/Williams 2019]
- Historical baseline: ~127,000 LGBTQ adults in Nevada as of 2022 reporting, ~9% of the state's 2020 electorate [source: https://glaad.org/releases/silver-state-equality-glaad-release-lgbtq-records-2022-nevada-candidates-call-media-include/ | 2022] — superseded by the 2023 Williams figure above.
- Nevada ranks among the higher-LGBT-share states (~10th; 6.5–6.6% of adults) [source: https://finance.yahoo.com/news/15-gayest-states-america-2024-212637339.html | 2024]

## 3. LGBTQ YOUTH — NEVADA YRBS + TREVOR PROJECT

### 3.1 National YRBS 2023 (context/benchmark)
- LGBQ+ students vs heterosexual: persistent sadness/hopelessness **65.7% vs 31.4%**; poor mental health **53.5% vs 21.5%**; seriously considered suicide **41.0% vs 13.0%**; attempted suicide **19.7% vs 6.0%** [source: https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/73/su/su7304a9.htm | 2024]
- 2023 national YRBS first included transgender identity: ~3% of high school students identify as transgender, +2% unsure [source: https://www.cdc.gov/healthy-youth/lgbtq-youth/health-disparities-among-lgbtq-youth.html | 2025]
- National YRBS 2023: 20% of all high school students seriously considered suicide; 9% attempted [source: https://www.cdc.gov/yrbs/dstr/pdf/YRBS-2023-Data-Summary-Trend-Report.pdf | 2024]

### 3.2 Nevada-specific youth data
- **The Nevada High School YRBS is administered by UNR School of Public Health on behalf of NV Dept. of Education and DPBH; statewide results live at ScholarWolf (2023: https://scholarwolf.unr.edu/handle/11714/10918)** [source: https://www.unr.edu/public-health/research-activities/nevada-youth-risk-behavior-survey | accessed 2026] — Nevada LGB-specific prevalence tables are in the state report; direct PDF extraction was blocked, so **Nevada-specific LGB percentages: NEEDS-VERIFICATION (pull from ScholarWolf or UNR request).**
- NV SB69 (2021) requires every district and charter school to participate in biennial YRBS [source: https://www.unr.edu/public-health/research-activities/nevada-youth-risk-behavior-survey | accessed 2026]
- **Trevor Project 2022 Nevada brief (Nevada-specific LGBTQ youth, N = NV respondents):** 44% of LGBTQ youth in Nevada seriously considered suicide in past year (75% among trans/nonbinary); 12% attempted (57% among trans/nonbinary); 64% wanted mental health care but could not get it (72% of trans/nonbinary); 37% said home is LGBTQ-affirming, 59% said school is; 49% said recent politics negatively impacted well-being; 50% experienced discrimination based on SO/GI [source: https://www.thetrevorproject.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/The-Trevor-Project-2022-National-Survey-on-LGBTQ-Youth-Mental-Health-by-State-Nevada.pdf | 2022]
- **Teen suicide mortality, Nevada: 13.4 per 100,000 ages 15–19 (2022–2024 pooled), rank 35th of 47 rated states (higher = worse); US average 9.7** [source: https://www.americashealthrankings.org/explore/measures/teen_suicide/NV | 2026, CDC WONDER]

## 7. NEVADA LGBTQ POLICY LANDSCAPE (MAP Equality Profile — verified in full)

**Overall policy score: 40.25/47 — "High"** (SO policy score 20.5/22; GI policy score 19.75/25); data as of Nov 11, 2026 [source: https://mapresearch.org/equality-profiles/nv/ | 2026]

**THRIVING (strengths):**
- Statewide nondiscrimination in employment, housing, public accommodations, credit/lending (SO & GI) — 100% of state population fully protected; no local gaps because coverage is statewide [source: https://mapresearch.org/equality-profiles/nv/ | 2026]
- **Conversion therapy banned** (SO & GI) [source: https://mapresearch.org/equality-profiles/nv/ | 2026]
- **No statewide ban on best-practice medical care for transgender youth** [source: https://mapresearch.org/equality-profiles/nv/ | 2026]
- **Medicaid covers transgender-related care; private insurers banned from excluding transgender care; state employee benefits trans-inclusive (0.5/0.5)** [source: https://mapresearch.org/equality-profiles/nv/ | 2026]
- Hate crime law covering LGBTQ people; panic-defense ban; anti-bullying + safe-school nondiscrimination; LGBTQ-inclusive curricular standards; second-parent adoption; marriage recognition; X markers on DL & birth certificate [source: https://mapresearch.org/equality-profiles/nv/ | 2026]
- No RFRA, no religious-exemption carve-outs (medical/child-welfare), no HIV criminalization flag, no drag restriction flag [source: https://mapresearch.org/equality-profiles/nv/ | 2026]

**STRUGGLING (deficits):**
- **"Negative law": forced outing of transgender youth in schools** (−0.25 penalized in MAP scoring) [source: https://mapresearch.org/equality-profiles/nv/ | 2026]
- **No transgender healthcare shield law** (0/1) [source: https://mapresearch.org/equality-profiles/nv/ | 2026]
- **No fertility treatment coverage in private insurance or Medicaid (0/1 and 0/0.5)**; no fertility-preservation coverage in Medicaid (0/0.5) [source: https://mapresearch.org/equality-profiles/nv/ | 2026]
- Jury service nondiscrimination covers SO but not GI (0/1 for GI) [source: https://mapresearch.org/equality-profiles/nv/ | 2026]
- Public opinion context: 85% of Nevadans favor laws protecting LGBTQ Americans from discrimination (PRRI 2023 AVA) [source: https://prri.org/research/views-on-lgbtq-rights-in-all-50-states/ | 2026 report of 2023 data]
