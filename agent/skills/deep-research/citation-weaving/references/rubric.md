# Citation Weaving Rubric

## Citation Formatting Standards
- Use readable inline Markdown links (`[NIST SP 800-207](https://...)`) right at the clause or table cell making the claim — never leave naked URLs or orphan `[1]` markers without clickable URLs.
- Every URL in `## Sources` must appear inline at least once, and every inline URL must appear in `## Sources` with title and date/publisher note.
