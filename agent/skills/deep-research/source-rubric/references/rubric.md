# Source Admissibility Rubric

## Evidentiary Rules
- Every load-bearing factual or numeric claim in the final report must trace to at least one Tier 1 source or two independent Tier 2 sources.
- Vendor self-reported metrics must be explicitly labeled with provenance and compared against independent measurements whenever available.
- Inaccessible paywalled or anti-bot-gated pages must never be bypassed or fabricated; note unavailability and locate open primary sources.
