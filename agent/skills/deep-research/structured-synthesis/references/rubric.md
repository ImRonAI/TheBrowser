# Structured Synthesis Rubric

## Synthesis vs. Compilation
- **Synthesize across sources**: Explain *why* approaches differ, *where* each wins, and *how* empirical numbers compare on a normalized basis.
- **Calibrated Language**: Match verb strength to confidence (`demonstrates` for High-confidence primary data; `suggests under workload X` for Moderate; `remains contested between A and B` for Contested).
