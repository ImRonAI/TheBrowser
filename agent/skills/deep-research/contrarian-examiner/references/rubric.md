# Contrarian Examiner Rubric

## Red-Team Attack Vectors
- **Selection & Survivorship Bias**: Are cited case studies cherry-picked winners while failed deployments go unmentioned?
- **Benchmark Gaming / Regime Mismatch**: Do laboratory benchmarks break down under production latency, cost, scale, or adversarial input?
- **Confounding Variables**: Does an observed correlation reflect macro shifts, pricing subsidies, or regulatory artifacts?

## Constructive Rigor
- Critique with **cited empirical counterevidence**, never unsupported cynicism. If a hypothesis survives serious falsification attempts, explicitly certify its resilience.
