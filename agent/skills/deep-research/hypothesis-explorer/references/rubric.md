# Hypothesis Explorer Rubric

## Falsifiability Standard
- Every hypothesis must specify: (a) causal mechanism or structural claim, (b) observable prediction, and (c) what specific empirical finding would refute it.
- Avoid consensus bias: include plausible minority or structural-risk explanations alongside mainstream narratives.

## Swarm Handoff Discipline
- Pass concise structured context in `handoff_to_agent` listing exact claims and URLs so `contrarian_examiner` attacks concrete premises rather than vague summaries.
- Converge within 1-2 handoff rounds; never loop indefinitely when uncertainty is genuinely irreducible.
