# Problem Framing Rubric

## Quality Criteria
- **Orthogonality**: Sub-questions must partition the inquiry space with minimal overlap so parallel investigation branches do not duplicate queries.
- **Falsifiability**: Phrase every sub-question so empirical evidence or primary documentation can confirm or refute candidate answers.
- **Temporal Anchoring**: Explicitly bind relative time expressions ("latest", "current", "recent") to concrete calendar years and dates discovered during reconnaissance.
- **Terminology Disambiguation**: Separate homonymous products, standards, tickers, and jurisdictions upfront.

## Required Output Sections
1. Core Research Objective (1-2 sentences)
2. Canonical Entities, Dates & Definitions
3. Decomposed Sub-Questions (`Q1` through `Q6`, ordered by evidentiary dependency)
4. Known Unknowns & Disconfirmation Targets
