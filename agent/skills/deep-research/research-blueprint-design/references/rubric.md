# Research Blueprint Design Rubric

## Branch Allocation Rules
- **Web Intelligence (`web_intelligence`)**: Assign canonical documentation, architectural specifications, whitepapers, standards bodies, and full-text article extraction tasks.
- **News & Data Intelligence (`news_data_intelligence`)**: Assign chronological event tracking, release notes, regulatory announcements, market/statistical lookups, and numeric cross-checks.
- **Adversarial Swarm (`adversarial_swarm`)**: Assign dominant hypotheses to stress-test, known failure modes, independent critiques, and conflicting empirical studies.
