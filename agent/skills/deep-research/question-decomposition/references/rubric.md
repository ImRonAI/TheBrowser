# Question Decomposition Rubric

## Decomposition Checklist
- Scale question count to complexity: 3-4 sub-questions for `standard` depth, 4-6 for `deep`, 6-8 for `exhaustive`.
- Avoid leading or presuppositional phrasing ("Why is X superior to Y?"); use neutral comparative phrasing ("On which workloads and cost envelopes does X outperform or underperform Y?").
- Ensure at least one sub-question explicitly targets edge cases, limitations, or contrarian evidence.
