# Quantitative Fact Lookup Rubric

## Numerical Integrity Rules
- **No Unanchored Numbers**: Every number must carry units, timeframe, and denominator/scope.
- **Cross-Check Material Outliers**: Any claim of >=2x improvement or dramatic market share shift requires checking methodology and at least one independent source.
- **Precision Honesty**: Keep reported significant figures faithful to the source; never invent decimal precision.
