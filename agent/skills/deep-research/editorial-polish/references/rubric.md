# Editorial Polish Rubric

## Editorial Non-Negotiables
- **Never Delete Citations**: Tightening prose must never drop inline `[Title](URL)` links or quantitative denominators/units.
- **Precision Over Brevity**: Eliminate filler phrases, never empirical substance or methodological caveats.
- **Clean GFM**: Headings (`#`, `##`, `###`), bullet lists, and pipe tables must follow strict GitHub-Flavored Markdown.
